# Your 25-pet gallery

The opening gallery now uses all 25 supplied Ampon Alaga profile cards, in the order provided. Each image is copied without alterations; full names, photos, details, and original branding remain visible. No ages or adoption availability are inferred as current.

The gallery crossfades every seven seconds and includes previous/next, pause/play, a pet counter, and a full-profile viewer with keyboard and close controls. It respects reduced-motion preferences and pauses while hovered, focused, or while a profile is open.

Upload index.html, styles.css, app.js, and the entire assets folder to your GitHub repository, then commit. Keep your existing config.js and Code.gs. The assets/pets subfolder must be preserved. This package has not been committed to the live repository.

The cream background and Playfair Display / Source Sans Pro typography are unchanged. The Instagram frame and the old three-photo gallery are no longer displayed.
