# Brand font and cream background update

Upload index.html and styles.css to the existing repository and commit the changes. Keep config.js unchanged. If you have not yet uploaded the November 1 update, upload app.js and the assets folder too.

Headings: Playfair Display, weight 600.
Body, navigation, labels, and controls: Source Sans Pro, weights 400 and 600.
Background: warm cream #faf7f0. Navy and bright blue brand colors are preserved.

Google Fonts are loaded over HTTPS. Georgia and Arial provide fallbacks if the font service is unavailable. The original logo is preserved.

For the Google-hosted version, replace Index.html in Apps Script and deploy a new version. Code.gs requires no change.
