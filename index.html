OUR SHARED BELIEF

Pets Are Family.

Care that feels like family.
Love that feels like home.
Where every pet belongs.

Every dog and cat deserves a loving home. Until they find one, they deserve to be fed, cared for, and given the chance to be known.

This belief brings Bites & Care and Ampon Alaga together. Our fundraiser starts with helping provide food, but our shared commitment goes further: helping more people see rescued pets as future family members.

CARE BEYOND THE COLLECTION

Provide food

Help provide food for rescued dogs and cats in Ampon Alaga’s care while they wait for families of their own.

Raise awareness

Share their stories and personalities so more people can get to know the pets waiting for a home.

Encourage adoption and fostering

Connect interested families with Ampon Alaga to learn about welcoming a rescued pet into their lives.

Educate pet parents

Help challenge misconceptions about Aspins, guide families as adopted pets settle in, and encourage a lifetime of responsible care.
