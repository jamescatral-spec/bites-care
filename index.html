Where every pet belongs

Why Ampon Alaga

Ampon Alaga helps rescued dogs and cats find loving homes. At Bites & Care, we believe every pet deserves the care and belonging that family brings.

We see an opportunity to support that work together, helping more people meet, understand, and welcome rescued pets into their lives.

A shared belief that every pet deserves a family.
